Quick troubleshooting

Performance issues

Restart the device, close background apps, and try again. Include device model and iOS version in your message.

No sound or haptics

Check system Silent Mode, volume, and in-app settings. Low Power Mode can reduce haptics on some devices.

Progress reset

If progress is stored locally, reinstalling the app or resetting device settings may remove local data.

Feedback and suggestions

I read every email personally. Feature ideas and improvement requests are welcome.
